**Semester**: Fall 2026

## Introduction

This Order Service Analytics app is a microservice for Algonquin Pet Store to take in orders from the queue on RabbitMQ and provide analytics. The Order Service Analytics will prvide details about orders that have been successfully completed and data about total sales, sales per products and top selling products.
